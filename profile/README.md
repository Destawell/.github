# Destawell

**Mobile-First Security Research | AI Red Teaming | Open-Source Tooling**

---

## Brand Profile

Destawell is a cybersecurity research brand founded by Niranj R. Mahaswar (Founder) and Shifana (Co-Founder). The brand operates at the intersection of Android penetration testing infrastructure, LLM safety validation, and automated mobile tooling. Primary environments include Termux, Kali NetHunter, and ARM64-based mobile red team deployments.

---

## Research & Disclosure

The Destawell research team identified a safety alignment bypass in Gemini 2.5 Pro related to CVE-2023-32233 — a Linux kernel race condition in nf_tables. Functional exploit primitives were generated by the model. Comparative testing showed refusal behaviors from Claude 3, GPT-4o, Llama 3, and GitHub Copilot.

- **Disclosure:** Google IssueTracker #889286
- **Reported to:** Google AI VRP
- **Final determination:** Marked out of scope
- **Documentation status:** Public
